## Test protocols

Do not compare two results unless the protocol is the same or the change is recorded.

### Strength and gymnastics

- **Push-ups:** record hand position, range standard, pacing/rest rule and stop rule. Current protocol `TBD`.
- **Strict pull-ups:** record grip, start/end standard, kipping exclusion and stop rule. Current protocol `TBD`.
- **Dead hang:** record grip, bar type, shoulder position and stop rule. Current protocol `TBD`.
- **Deadlift:** do not infer a current maximum from the historical 180 kg. Current assessment method and readiness criteria are `TBD`.
- **Muscle-ups:** establish usable shoulder range, scapular control, strict pulling strength, hollow/arch control, controlled kip volume and transition practice before testing repeated full reps. Exact thresholds are `TBD`.

### Aerobic and real-world capacity

- **2,000 m row:** record time, average watts, average and maximum heart rate when available, pacing, RPE and next-day response. The first return test should be hard but need not be maximal.
- **5 km run:** retain as a later benchmark. Timing, route/surface and readiness criteria are `TBD` while impact tolerance is rebuilt.
- **Adventure assessment:** begin with an approximately 90-minute low-to-moderate outing. Record modality, duration, approximate intensity, state at 60 and 90 minutes, next-morning fatigue/stiffness and any limiting body part.

### Body, balance and recovery

- **Body composition:** repeat the same Jackson–Pollock 3-site method under similar conditions about every eight weeks. The recorded sites are chest, abdomen and thigh; use the same side, operator technique, device and time of day. Exact landmark details and device remain `TBD`.
- **Static balance:** eyes closed, single leg. Record surface, footwear, arm rule, attempt count, cap and reason the trial ended. Existing results lack these details.
- **Sleep:** record bedtime, wake time, estimated duration and morning energy on a 1–5 scale. Resting heart rate is optional.
- **Caffeine experiment:** the working hypothesis is that no caffeine after 12:00 improves sleep and training consistency. Initial test duration is two weeks; start date and result are `TBD`.

## Review rule

Use the scorecard to decide what to learn next, not to manufacture certainty. Record raw results in the ledger first, then interpretation and decisions in a dated review.
